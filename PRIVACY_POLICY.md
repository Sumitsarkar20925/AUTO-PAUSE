# Privacy Policy for Auto-Pause Videos Extension

**Last Updated**: May 2, 2026

## Overview

The Auto-Pause Videos extension ("Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage.

## Data Collection

The Auto-Pause Videos extension:

- **Does NOT collect** any personal data
- **Does NOT track** your browsing history
- **Does NOT send** any data to external servers
- **Does NOT use** analytics or telemetry
- **Does NOT store** any user information
- **Does NOT use** cookies or local storage

## How the Extension Works

The extension operates entirely on your device and:

1. Detects when a tab becomes hidden (you switch to another tab)
2. Detects when the browser window becomes inactive (you minimize or switch windows)
3. Pauses any active video elements on the hidden/inactive tab
4. Makes no external requests or connections

All functionality is local to your browser and no data leaves your device.

## Permissions Explanation

The extension requests the following permissions from Chrome:

### `permissions: ["storage"]`
- **Purpose**: Saves whether auto-pause is enabled or disabled
- **What it means**: Stores a simple on/off preference in Chrome sync storage
- **Limitation**: Does not store browsing history or page content

## What We Don't Do

- ❌ We don't collect URLs or page content
- ❌ We don't track which sites you visit
- ❌ We don't store any browsing data

- ❌ We don't use third-party services
